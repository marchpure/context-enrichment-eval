# AsterDesk list-price sheet — APPROVED, effective 2026-11-01
Currency: USD. Meter: processed document pages per calendar month. Included allowance: 7000 pages/month. Overage rate: 1.30 USD per 1,000 pages. Overage is pro rata with no 1,000-page block rounding. These rates are synthetic test data.

## Calculation
Only pages above the included allowance are billable overage. A customer below its allowance has zero overage. Convert the per-1,000 rate to the actual billable quantity before multiplying. An allowance is a quantity, not a monetary credit. This list-price model does not specify taxes, minimum invoices, currency conversion or outage credits.

## Scope
This page meter is unrelated to the full-export document count and unrelated to support acknowledgement minutes. Product migrations require an approved agreement before transferring balances. A draft proposed bulk-export tariff is not covered by this page-processing sheet. If a user asks for that price, do not substitute this overage rate.

## Version control
Use the policy effective on the event date. This sheet replaces the former list-price sheet for current periods; archived invoices may retain historical prices. Commercial Operations has not published a bulk-export tariff or a reimbursement formula in the current corpus.
