# Incident handling vocabulary and handoff — APPROVED
P1 means the highest-impact incident class. Severity is determined by impact, independently of plan. A Basic subscriber can experience a P1; that does not create an uncontracted P1 support SLA. The service policy gives acknowledgement and escalation targets; neither is a fixed time-to-recovery promise.

## Three different milestones
Acknowledgement means the support team has accepted the report. Ownership means a named incident commander is responsible. Mitigation reduces impact. Resolution closes the incident after verification. A report acknowledged inside the target may still require a long investigation. A draft recovery objective is not a contractual resolution deadline.

## No-owner escalation
Evaluate the product's no-owner timer, not the acknowledgement timer. If the no-owner threshold is reached, route to the incident commander whether or not a human acknowledgement has already been sent. Preserve the original report time and record the coverage rule used. Do not reset the timer by creating a duplicate ticket.

## Business-hours examples
The standard Business coverage in this fixture is Monday-Friday 09:00-18:00 Asia/Shanghai, excluding public holidays. A Saturday report begins covered SLA time at Monday 09:00 when no holiday intervenes. Enterprise coverage is stated per product. Follow-the-sun on-call handoffs do not change an approved coverage window. A customer exception must explicitly apply to support coverage before it can alter this calculation.
