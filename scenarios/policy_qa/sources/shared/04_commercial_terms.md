# Commercial interpretation guide — APPROVED
The product price sheet gives its currency, billing unit, included quantity and overage rate. Do not compare a per-1,000-unit number with a per-unit number without converting. Overage quantities are max(actual minus included, zero). This fixture does not impose block rounding unless an operative product sheet says so. Tax and reimbursement are not implied by list prices.

## Quotes and drafts
A proposed bulk-export price in an unapproved draft is not an available tariff. A quote with a missing approved rate stays undecided; a support SLA or product list price cannot fill that gap. Credits, refunds and special procurement terms need explicit evidence. A transcript asking Finance to investigate a price is not an approved price decision.

## Change control
The effective date selects a version. Historical tables remain relevant for historical invoices but cannot override the current sheet. A migration to a different product preserves no numeric entitlement unless a named migration agreement explicitly says so. This corpus specifies list-price calculations only, not legally binding customer invoices or financial advice.
